Privacy Notice

Information processed

Career-path comparisons are stored in your browser’s local storage. CareerShield does not receive them until you ask the AI Guide a question. When you use the AI Guide, your question and up to four comparison records are sent through a CareerShield Netlify Function to the OpenAI API to generate an answer.

School and occupation searches are sent through CareerShield’s Netlify Functions to College Scorecard and O*NET. Payment details and purchase contact information are collected and processed by Stripe; CareerShield does not receive your full card number.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used to operate searches, generate requested AI explanations, process purchases, deliver reports, prevent abuse, and support customers. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide those functions. CareerShield does not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

You can remove saved comparisons using “Clear comparison” or your browser controls. Do not enter Social Security numbers, account credentials, medical information, precise military operational information, or other sensitive data into the comparison or AI Guide. Purchase records may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations. Privacy or deletion requests may be sent using the contact information on your Stripe receipt.
