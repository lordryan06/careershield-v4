Privacy Notice

Information processed

Signed-out career-path comparisons are stored in your browser’s local storage. If you create a CareerShield account, Netlify Identity processes your name, email address, password credentials, and account session. While you are signed in, up to four comparison records are stored in a private, account-specific Netlify Blobs record so you can retrieve them on another device.

When you use the AI Guide, your question and up to four comparison records are sent through a CareerShield Netlify Function to the OpenAI API to generate an answer. School and occupation searches are sent through CareerShield’s Netlify Functions to official data services. Payment details are processed by Stripe; CareerShield does not receive your full card number.

Partner Review sharing

Partner Review is not currently accepting purchases. Before it opens, customers will receive a clear consent notice identifying what comparison, report, account contact information, and review notes will be shared with the assigned approved independent counselor or partner. Reviewers will receive only the information needed to complete the requested review and will be subject to CareerShield access, confidentiality, retention, and quality requirements.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used to authenticate accounts, synchronize saved plans, operate searches, generate requested AI explanations, process and display purchases, deliver requested products, prevent abuse, and support customers. Service providers and an assigned Partner Review reviewer may process information only as needed for those functions. CareerShield does not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

You can remove synchronized comparisons using “Clear comparison” or “Restart.” Signed-out device copies can also be removed through browser controls. Account or privacy deletion requests may be submitted through the support form below. Do not enter Social Security numbers, account credentials, medical information, precise military operational information, or other sensitive data into CareerShield. Purchase and review records may be retained as required for fulfillment, quality control, accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations.

Deep Analysis is an instant digital product. If CareerShield cannot generate or make the purchased analysis available, you may request correction or a full refund. After successful generation, contact CareerShield within seven calendar days if the analysis is missing promised sections or contains a material processing error; CareerShield will correct it or evaluate an appropriate refund.

AI Guide and Deep Analysis Disclosure

The CareerShield Guide and paid Deep Analysis generate responses using an OpenAI model. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. These tools explain and stress-test the saved comparison, challenge assumptions, and identify facts to verify; they do not independently or conclusively verify live costs, laws, eligibility rules, benefits, recruiting promises, credentials, program status, or employment outcomes.

Your question or purchased-analysis request and comparison context are transmitted to OpenAI’s API. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in, and that abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for a limited period. Do not include sensitive personal information. Always verify consequential decisions with authoritative sources and qualified professionals.

CareerShield Partner Review

Partner Review is the planned $49.99 premium tier and is not currently accepting purchases. CareerShield will generate the underlying comparison, evidence record, scoring, 10-year model, AI analysis, flagged assumptions, and formatted report.

An approved independent counselor or partner will review the ready-made case using CareerShield’s standardized checklist. The reviewer’s role is to assess whether paths are represented correctly, consider context, review material assumptions and discrepancies, identify important risks, add comments and recommendations, and approve or return the report for correction. The reviewer is not expected to write the report from scratch.

Reviewer qualifications and the scope of any professional credentials will be disclosed accurately. Approval means the defined Partner Review process was completed; it is not a guarantee, endorsement of a school or employer, or financial, legal, admissions, recruiting, military, employment, or other regulated advice. CareerShield will initially consider independent counselors and private counseling organizations; compensation arrangements involving school employees require separate employer-policy, ethics, and legal review.
