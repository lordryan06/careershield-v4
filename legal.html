Privacy Notice

Information processed

Signed-out career-path comparisons are stored in your browser’s local storage. If you create a CareerShield account, Netlify Identity processes your name, email address, password credentials, and account session. While you are signed in, up to four comparison records are stored in a private, account-specific Netlify Blobs record so you can retrieve them on another device.

When you use the AI Guide, your question and up to four comparison records are sent through a CareerShield Netlify Function to the OpenAI API to generate an answer. School and occupation searches are sent through CareerShield’s Netlify Functions to College Scorecard and O*NET. Payment details and purchase contact information are collected and processed by Stripe; CareerShield does not receive your full card number. After a signed-in report purchase, Stripe sends CareerShield the order status, amount, currency, checkout identifier, payment identifier, purchase email, and date so the purchase can appear in your account dashboard.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used to authenticate accounts, synchronize saved plans, operate searches, generate requested AI explanations, process and display purchases, deliver reports, prevent abuse, and support customers. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide those functions. CareerShield does not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

You can remove synchronized comparisons using “Clear comparison” or “Restart.” Signed-out device copies can also be removed through browser controls. Account or privacy deletion requests may be submitted through the support form below. Do not enter Social Security numbers, account credentials, medical information, precise military operational information, or other sensitive data into the comparison or AI Guide. Purchase records may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations.

Personalized Report Refund Policy

You may request a full refund before personalized review work begins. If CareerShield cannot deliver the purchased report, you will receive a full refund. Once review work has begun, cancellation requests are evaluated based on work completed. After delivery, contact CareerShield within seven calendar days if the report is missing promised sections or contains a material processing error; CareerShield will correct the report or provide an appropriate refund.

Submit refund requests through the support form below and include the purchase email and date. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; bank processing times vary. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

AI Guide Disclosure

The CareerShield Guide generates responses using an OpenAI model. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. The Guide is designed to explain the comparison supplied to it, challenge assumptions, and suggest facts to verify; it does not independently verify live costs, laws, eligibility rules, benefits, recruiting promises, or program outcomes.

Your AI question and comparison context are transmitted to OpenAI’s API. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in, and that abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for a limited period. Do not include sensitive personal information. Always verify consequential decisions with authoritative sources and qualified professionals.
