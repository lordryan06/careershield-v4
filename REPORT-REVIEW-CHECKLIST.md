# CareerShield Partner Review Checklist

Complete this checklist for every $49.99 CareerShield Partner Review. CareerShield supplies the completed analysis and evidence package; the assigned approved independent counselor or partner reviews the case, adds contextual judgment, and approves it or returns it for correction. Keep the completed checklist with the report record.

## Data check

- [ ] Career paths match the customer’s selections.
- [ ] School and program names match the supplied information.
- [ ] Career and O*NET mappings are reasonable and disclosed.
- [ ] Tuition and training costs have source or assumption labels.
- [ ] User-entered assumptions are identified.
- [ ] Government-source data is identified with dates.
- [ ] Missing and unverified information is identified.
- [ ] Each material value includes source status, direct URL, data year/vintage, access date, confidence, and limitations.
- [ ] Planned integrations are not described as verified sources.
- [ ] Conflicting source values are preserved, explained, and resolved or disclosed.
- [ ] College paths are checked against the current official catalog; IPEDS and College Scorecard are used for their appropriate scopes.
- [ ] Labor-market wages, employment, and projections are cross-checked against BLS when available.
- [ ] Credential claims are checked against the issuer and Credential Engine when available.
- [ ] Apprenticeship registration and sponsor claims are checked with Apprenticeship.gov/RAPIDS, the state agency, or sponsor as applicable.
- [ ] License requirements are checked with CareerOneStop or the responsible state board when applicable.
- [ ] Military and GI Bill claims use the appropriate DFAS, DoD, service, O*NET, and VA sources.

## Financial model

- [ ] Training duration is represented correctly.
- [ ] Earnings during training are included where applicable.
- [ ] Debt and financing drag are represented correctly.
- [ ] Starting earnings are sourced or clearly labeled assumptions.
- [ ] Ten-year calculations are internally consistent.
- [ ] Military benefits are separated from cash compensation.
- [ ] GI Bill value is treated as an education benefit, not current cash earnings.
- [ ] Material opportunity costs are represented.

## Risk review

- [ ] Employment-demand interpretation is supported and qualified.
- [ ] AI-resilience language is appropriately qualified.
- [ ] Career-flexibility reasoning follows from the available information.
- [ ] The report contains no unsupported guarantees.
- [ ] The downside scenario is reasonable and explained.

## Action plan

- [ ] Important assumptions are flagged.
- [ ] Questions requiring verification are included.
- [ ] Next steps are specific to the customer’s paths.
- [ ] The report makes no financial, legal, recruiting, admissions, military, employment, or career-counseling guarantees.

## Partner context and judgment

- [ ] The reviewer considered the student or family context supplied with the case without inventing missing personal facts.
- [ ] Material assumptions that appear unreasonable or unusually optimistic are identified.
- [ ] Source discrepancies and unresolved verification gaps are addressed in reviewer comments.
- [ ] The reviewer added concise comments, recommendations, and questions rather than rewriting the AI-generated report from scratch.
- [ ] The reviewer stayed within the disclosed scope of their qualifications and did not imply credentials they do not hold.

## Final review

- [ ] The report is understandable.
- [ ] Numbers and labels are consistent throughout.
- [ ] Sources and source dates are identified.
- [ ] All unresolved limitations are prominent.
- [ ] No sensitive personal information appears unnecessarily.
- [ ] The report is ready for delivery.
