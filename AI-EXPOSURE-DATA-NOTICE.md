# AI exposure data notice

`ai-exposure-openai.csv` is the occupation-level dataset published in the OpenAI repository **GPTs are GPTs: An Early Look at the Labor Market Impact Potential of Large Language Models**.

- Source: https://github.com/openai/GPTs-are-GPTs
- Dataset path: `data/occ_level.csv`
- License: MIT License, as published with the source repository
- CareerShield field used: `dv_rating_beta` (`E1 + 0.5 × E2` task-exposure measure)

CareerShield uses this research measure as one component of AI Resilience Model v1.0. Occupational AI exposure describes the share of tasks that language models may affect. It does not measure or predict whether an occupation will be eliminated.
